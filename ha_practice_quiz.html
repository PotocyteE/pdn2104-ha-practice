L3 Nutrition / BMI / WHR

SQ: Calculate BMI for 80 kg / 1.75 m and give WHO category + Chinese term.

BMI ≈ 26.1 = Overweight (超重). <18.5 體重過輕, 18.5–24.9 normal, 25–29.9 超重, ≥30 肥胖.

L4_1 Thyroid Palpation

SQ: Posterior approach steps + normal findings + Chinese term.

Stand behind. Head forward & right. Left hand pushes trachea right. Right fingers between trachea & SCM. Swallow – feel movement. Reverse. Normal often not palpable (甲狀腺). If felt: rubbery smooth. Abnormal: enlarged/nodules/bruit.

L4_2 Confrontation Test

SQ: Steps + normal angles.

Eye level 2 ft. Cover opposite eyes. Wiggling finger from periphery. Client says “now” when seen (match examiner). Temporal start behind. Angles: sup 50°, temp 90°, inf 70°, nasal 60°. Abnormal = peripheral loss (青光眼).

L4_3 Whispered Voice

SQ: Procedure, masking, scoring + Chinese.

2 ft behind. Mask by pushing tragus. Exhale, whisper 3 numbers/letters. If wrong, different set. Pass 4/6. Other ear new set. High-frequency loss (耳語試驗).

L5 Respiratory

SQ: Symmetric chest expansion (posterior) + tactile fremitus normal vs abnormal.

Hands posterolateral, thumbs T9–T10 to spine. Deep breath – thumbs move apart symmetrically. Fremitus: “ninety-nine” with ulnar edge. Symmetric normal. ↑ consolidation, ↓ emphysema/effusion.

L6 CVS

SQs: PQRST questions for chest pain; steps to measure JVP + normal; abdominojugular test positive finding.

PQRST: Provoking, Quality, Region/radiation, Severity 0–10, Timing. JVP: 30–45°, from sternal angle, normal <3 cm. Abdominojugular: RUQ pressure 30–60s → sustained JVP rise = right heart failure. Carotid: one side at a time, gentle.

L7 Abdomen

SQs: Fluid Wave, Murphy’s, Rebound, Cullen’s + Chinese terms & positive findings.

Fluid Wave (波動試驗): midline hand, strike left flank, feel wave = ascites. Murphy’s (墨菲氏徵): inspiratory arrest under right costal margin = cholecystitis. Rebound (反跳痛): pain on release = peritonitis. Cullen’s: bluish umbilicus = bleeding.

L9 MSK

SQs: Phalen, Tinel, Bulge, Ballottement, SLR + positive findings & Chinese.

Phalen (法倫試驗): 90° flexed 60s → numbness = CTS. Tinel: percuss wrist → tingling. Bulge: fluid wave after stroking = small effusion. Ballottement: patella floats = larger effusion. SLR 30–70° radiating pain = sciatica.
