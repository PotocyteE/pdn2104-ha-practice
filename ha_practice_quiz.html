Lecture 3 – BMI & WHR

A client weighs 80 kg and is 1.75 m tall. What is the BMI and classification (WHO)?

Short Question Practice: Describe how to calculate BMI and state the WHO categories. Include one Chinese term.

Model: BMI = weight(kg) / height(m)². Categories: <18.5 underweight (體重過輕), 18.5–24.9 normal, 25–29.9 overweight (超重), 30–39.9 obesity (肥胖), ≥40 extreme obesity.

Lecture 4_1 – Thyroid Palpation

Short Question: Describe the steps of the posterior approach for palpating the thyroid gland. Include normal findings and one Chinese term.

Model: Stand behind client. Ask to sit straight then bend head slightly forward and to the right. Use left hand to push trachea slightly right. Curve right fingers between trachea and sternomastoid. Ask client to swallow; thyroid moves up under fingers. Reverse for other side. Normal adult thyroid often not palpable (甲狀腺). If felt, rubbery and smooth. Abnormal: enlarged, tender, nodules.

Lecture 4_2 – Confrontation Test

Short Question: Outline the steps of the confrontation test for visual fields. Include normal angle ranges.

Model: Position at eye level ~2 feet away. Client covers one eye with opaque card; examiner covers opposite eye. Test uncovered eye. Advance wiggling finger from periphery in several directions. Client says “now” when first seen (should match examiner). Temporal start slightly behind. Normal: ~50° superior, 90° temporal, 70° inferior, 60° nasal. Abnormal: peripheral field loss.

Lecture 4_3 – Whispered Voice Test

Short Question: Describe the whispered voice test procedure, including masking and scoring.

Model: Stand 2 feet behind. Mask non-test ear by pushing tragus in/out. Exhale fully then whisper 3 random numbers/letters (e.g. 5, B, 6). If wrong, repeat different set. Pass = 4 of 6 correct. Test other ear with new set. Detects high-frequency loss (耳語試驗).

Lecture 5 – Respiratory

Short Question: Describe how to assess for symmetric chest expansion (posterior). What is the normal finding?

Model: Place both hands sideways on posterolateral chest wall with thumbs pointing toward spine. Ask client to take a deep breath. Normal: thumbs move apart symmetrically. Abnormal: lag or asymmetric expansion.

Lecture 7 – Abdomen

Short Question: Describe the Fluid Wave test for ascites, including positioning and positive finding. Include Chinese term.

Model: Stand on client’s right. Client places ulnar edge of hand firmly on midline of abdomen. Examiner places left hand on right flank and strikes left flank firmly with right hand. Positive: distinct fluid wave felt → ascites (波動試驗). Negative if gas or fat.

Lecture 9 – Musculoskeletal

Short Question: Describe the Phalen test and Tinel sign for carpal tunnel syndrome. What constitutes a positive result?

Model: Phalen: Hold hands back-to-back, wrists flexed 90° for 60 seconds. Positive: numbness and burning. Tinel: Percuss median nerve at wrist. Positive: burning and tingling. Both indicate carpal tunnel syndrome (腕隧道症候群).
